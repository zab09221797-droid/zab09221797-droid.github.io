Chengdu Travel Guide 2026: A Foreigner's Essential Handbook

Last updated: June 2026 · 6 min read

Chengdu is the heartbeat of Sichuan province. Whether you're here for the giant pandas, the spicy hotpot, or the laid-back teahouse culture, navigating this city is easier than you think.

I. Core Documents

Your passport is your "universal ID." Always carry it. From high-speed train stations to national parks, your passport and valid visa are the only documents you need to prove your identity.

PRACTICAL INFO

Keep digital copies of your passport and visa on your phone. If you lose your passport, having these photos will drastically speed up the process at your embassy.

II. Chengdu Highlights

The city blends ancient history with modern urban life. From the bustling Wuhou Shrine to the tranquil parks, there is plenty to explore.

Explore local culture and breathtaking nature near Chengdu.

III. Food & Navigation

Chengdu is a UNESCO City of Gastronomy. Look for the traditional wooden signs to find authentic local dishes.
