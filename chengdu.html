Chengdu Travel Guide 2026: A Foreigner's Essential Handbook

Last updated: June 2026 · 10 min read

Chengdu is the perfect blend of slow-paced teahouse culture and high-speed modern development. For many international travelers, it's the highlight of their China trip. This guide strips away the complexity and gives you the exact tools you need.

I. Core Documents

Your passport is your "universal ID." In China, hotels, trains, and even some scenic spots are linked to your passport number. Do not rely on digital photos alone; always keep the original document in a secure, waterproof sleeve.

PRO-TIP: EMERGENCY BACKUP

Keep a physical copy of your visa and passport main page in your suitcase, and a high-resolution scan on your secure cloud storage. If your passport is lost, the local embassy will require these details to issue a temporary travel document.

II. Exploring Chengdu

Chengdu is massive. Whether you are wandering through the history of Wuhou Shrine or getting lost in the green mountain forests, every turn offers a unique perspective of Sichuan.

From the historic temple paths to breathtaking mountain lakes, Chengdu offers diverse experiences.

III. Dining and Local Lifestyle

Sichuan food is more than just "spicy." It is an art form. When you see traditional wooden menu signs hanging in the old streets, you've found the real deal.

IV. Essential Travel Checklist

  • Booking: Use international platforms like Trip.com for hotel and attraction bookings.
  • Transport: Use Alipay or WeChat Pay if possible, but keep 200 RMB in cash for small vendors.
  • Communication: Download a reliable VPN or an e-SIM solution for your phone before landing.
