About This Site

China Travel Guide

China Travel Guide is an independent travel resource built for foreign visitors planning trips to China. The site covers practical topics that most travel guides gloss over — how to actually get a SIM card, why your WeChat Pay setup failed, which section of the Great Wall is worth the trip and which isn't.

The guides on this site are written from firsthand experience traveling and living in China. The goal is to give honest, specific information rather than the kind of generic advice that sounds helpful but doesn't actually tell you anything useful when you're standing outside the Forbidden City without a booking.

Coverage includes Beijing, Shanghai, Chengdu, Xi'an and a growing number of other destinations, plus practical guides on payments, transport, visas, SIM cards and internet access.

The site is updated regularly to reflect current conditions — visa rules, booking procedures and practical details change, and we try to keep the information accurate and current.
